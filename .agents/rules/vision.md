10. AI Assistant (PageAgent)

I actually think this is a better fit than integrating a hosted LLM directly.

Instead of a floating chatbot that invents answers, use PageAgent to reason over the content already present on each page.

Examples:

On a Status Meanings page:

i would like you to find the common questions on line especially on facebook -
"Summarise this page."

"What should I do next?"

"Explain this in simple English."

On a Grant Guide:

"Do I qualify if I receive UIF?"

"Which documents do I need?"

On a Payment Dates page:

"When is the next payment?"

"Why are payment dates different for grants?"

The AI stays grounded in your published content, reducing maintenance and helping users navigate long pages.
