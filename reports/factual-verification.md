## 1. Fact-Checking Standards & Verification Protocol

SassaGrantHub enforces a strict primary-source verification standard:
1. **Primary Legislative & Official Directives Only:** No official procedure, grant amount, threshold, or payment date is published based solely on competitor claims, informal social media posts, or unverified blog articles.
2. **Standardized Verification Labels:**
   - **CONFIRMED:** Directly supported by official Government Gazettes, Department of Social Development directives, or official SASSA statements.
   - **HISTORICAL:** Verified historical facts (such as the former R350 grant amount) accurately identified as past policies.
   - **EXPECTED:** Projected payment windows or upcoming budget proposals explicitly disclosed as estimates rather than confirmed announcements.
   - **UNVERIFIED:** Information undergoing secondary check; excluded from public guides until confirmed.

## 3. Corrections of Misleading Claims

During our comprehensive audit, several common industry misconceptions and historical inaccuracies were verified and corrected:
1. **Misconception: R350 Grant Amount:** Competitor articles frequently mix R350 and R370, confusing beneficiaries about their payout amount. SassaGrantHub clearly explains that R350 was the historical baseline, raised to R370 by ministerial determination.
2. **Misconception: Third-Party Bank Accounts Allowed:** Unscrupulous blogs suggest beneficiaries can submit a family member's bank account. We clearly cite SASSA regulations prohibiting third-party bank accounts to protect applicants from having their verification blocked by PASANet.
3. **Misconception: Gold Cards Have Expired:** Competitors published misleading articles claiming Gold Cards expired on past dates. We confirmed with Postbank that cards remain operational while new Black cards are distributed.
4. **Misconception: Re-applying Automatically Overrides a Decline:** Many beneficiaries believe re-applying fixes a declined month. We clarify that each month is evaluated independently and a decline can only be reversed through a formal ITSAA appeal lodged within 90 days.

## 4. Central Registers Established

To maintain ongoing factual integrity, two machine-readable data registers have been built into the project repository:
- `data/editorial-source-registry.json`: Full catalogue of primary authorities, URLs, authority levels, and verification methods.
- `data/factual-claims-register.json`: Line-by-line inventory of all statutory claims, amounts, thresholds, and their associated site URLs.
