## 3. Publisher Identity & Government Disclaimers

AdSense strictly penalizes websites that mislead users regarding government affiliation:
- **Header & Footer Disclaimers:** Every page prominently displays: *"SASSA Grant Hub is an independent information publication and is not affiliated with the South African Social Security Agency (SASSA) or the South African Government."*
- **Direct Official Links:** Every guide clearly provides external links to official government portals (`srd.sassa.gov.za`, `www.sassa.gov.za`, `srd.dsd.gov.za`) so beneficiaries can easily distinguish independent advice from official government submission portals.
- **Editorial & Corrections Policy:** A dedicated `/editorial-policy` and `/corrections-policy` explain fact-checking standards, author backgrounds, and public correction mechanisms, directly satisfying Google's E-E-A-T criteria.

---

## 4. Preventing Low-Value Content Penalties

Google AdSense frequently rejects social grant blogs for "Low-Value Content" or "Thin Content". SassaGrantHub eliminates this risk through:
1. **The 4-Pillar Status Framework:** Instead of thin 100-word definitions, every status page provides comprehensive operational guidance (What it means, What could cause it, What to check, What to do next).
2. **Interactive Tools Suite:** 12 client-side tools, headlined by the new **Beneficiary Problem Solver**, provide immediate functional utility beyond static articles.
3. **Primary Source Registries:** All claims are verified against official Government Gazettes, demonstrating authoritative research.
4. **Zero Duplicate Route Expansion:** R350 and R370 queries are handled on a single canonical page; status codes are organized into distinct intents without artificial page duplication.

## 5. Monetization Recommendations & Action Plan

1. **Ad Placement Strategy:** Maintain `<AdUnit />` placements between major editorial sections and below the problem solver, ensuring ads complement rather than interrupt the reading flow.
2. **Review Submission:** The site is fully prepared for formal Google AdSense account site review.
3. **Ongoing Monitoring:** Periodically verify that third-party ad networks do not inject unvetted loan scams or predatory financial products targeting vulnerable grant recipients.
