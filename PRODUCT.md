# Product

## Register

product

## Users

South African citizens and residents seeking SASSA social grant information: applicants checking eligibility, recipients verifying payment dates and status meanings, caregivers managing child support grants, elderly individuals navigating older person pensions, appeal filers tracking deadlines, and general audiences wanting to understand the social security system. Users are often on mobile devices, may have limited data budgets, and need clear, authoritative, empathetic information they can trust. Many arrive from search engines with a specific question; the site must answer that question immediately and then guide them to related content.

## Product Purpose

An independent, premium editorial resource for South African social grant information. The site is the product — a destination where content, layout, typography, imagery, and navigation work together to educate and guide the reader. Success means visitors feel they've read a trustworthy, well-designed publication, not navigated a directory or government portal. Every page should satisfy multiple related search intents while encouraging natural onward exploration through contextual recommendations and internal linking.

## Brand Personality

Earnest · Grounded · Accessible

The voice is authoritative without being bureaucratic, warm without being casual. Like a trusted friend who happens to know the system inside out. Clarity and empathy are the default; jargon is explained, processes are demystified, and the reader is never made to feel silly for asking.

## Anti-references

- Government portals (bureaucratic, dense, low-trust visual language)
- Documentation sites / wikis (structure-exposing navigation, link-heavy, no reading flow)
- Directory sites (lists of links, no editorial voice)
- AI-generated landing pages (generic, no POV, template-feeling)
- Generic SASSA information sites (low production quality, untrustworthy)
- The benchmark is the reading quality of publications like Investopedia, DSD News, and GOV.UK's content clarity, adapted to the South African context

## Design Principles

1. **Comprehension first** — Every design decision must answer: does this improve understanding? Layout, typography, and imagery exist to make the information clearer, not to decorate.

2. **Trust through craft** — Premium production values (deliberate spacing, intentional typography, thoughtful imagery) signal authority and reliability. The site should feel expensive and editorial, not quick and scrappy.

3. **Frictionless reading** — Navigation fades into the background; the content is the interface. Users should be able to arrive, read, understand, and explore with the fewest possible clicks. A visitor should enjoy spending 5–10 minutes on a page.

4. **Contextual discovery** — Every page is a destination that satisfies multiple intents. Internal links, recommendations, and related content are woven into the reading experience, not dumped in sidebar lists.

5. **Empathetic accessibility** — Design for low-bandwidth, mobile-first, high-trust. No decorative excess, no data-heavy animations that drain batteries. Clear contrast, readable type, and generous touch targets are not checkboxes — they are the foundation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum target
- Mobile-first responsive design (many users on budget smartphones)
- High contrast text (no light gray on light backgrounds)
- Generous touch targets (minimum 44px)
- Reduced motion support for all animations
- South African English as primary language; plain language for low-literacy users
